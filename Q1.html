Tim Berners-Lee

Sir Tim Berners-Lee is the British computer scientist who invented the World Wide Web. In 1989, while he was working at CERN in Switzerland, he came up with a way for researchers to share documents by linking them together over the internet. By the end of 1990 he had built the first web browser, web server and website. Instead of patenting it and making money off it, he made the web free for anyone to use, which is a big part of why it took off. He went on to found the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) in 1994, the group that still sets the standards for HTML and CSS, so in a way he's the reason this assignment exists.
